# Supported Models (`SM-P619`)

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL WARNING:**  
> Samsung released four different versions of the Galaxy Tab S6 Lite across 2020, 2022, and 2024 with completely different processors. **Flashing files built for the wrong model will hard-brick your tablet.**

---

## ✅ Supported Model

| Model Number | Release Year | Variant | Codename | Chipset | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SM-P619** | 2022 | **LTE** | `gta4xlvelte` | Snapdragon 720G | **Supported (Target Device)** |

---

## ❌ Unsupported Models (Do Not Flash)

| Model Number | Release Year | Variant | Codename | Chipset | Why It Won't Work |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SM-P613** | 2022 | Wi-Fi | `gta4xlvewifi` | Snapdragon 720G | Wi-Fi only (no LTE modem hardware — flash the base ROM directly without this patcher) |
| **SM-P610** | 2020 | Wi-Fi | `gta4xlwifi` | Exynos 9611 | Incompatible Exynos hardware (**will hard-brick**) |
| **SM-P615** | 2020 | LTE | `gta4xl` | Exynos 9611 | Incompatible Exynos hardware (**will hard-brick**) |
| **SM-P620** | 2024 | Wi-Fi | `gta4xlveu` | Exynos 1280 | Incompatible Exynos hardware (**will hard-brick**) |
| **SM-P625** | 2024 | LTE | `gta4xlveulte` | Exynos 1280 | Incompatible Exynos hardware (**will hard-brick**) |

---

## 🔍 How to Check Your Model Number

1. Open **Settings** on your tablet.
2. Scroll down and tap **About tablet**.
3. Look at **Model name** — it must say **`SM-P619`** (or **`SM-P619N`**).
